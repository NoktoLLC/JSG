About JSG

A Master Craftsman Dedicated to Excellence

Joshua Grodin isn't just a contractor — he's a true craftsman who takes immense pride in every cut, every joint, every finish.

Since founding JSG Contracting & Remodeling, LLC in 2009, Joshua has built a reputation that speaks for itself. With over 15 years leading JSG in Nashua, NH, his work has earned consistent 5-star reviews from homeowners and deep respect from his peers in the trades.

But what truly sets Joshua apart is his hands-on approach. He's not managing from a desk — he's on-site, personally ensuring every detail meets his exacting standards. From initial concept through final walkthrough, you're working directly with the person building your home.
